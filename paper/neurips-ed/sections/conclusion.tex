JaxARC provides the first accelerator-native environment for ARC, making large-scale reinforcement learning research practical on this domain. By reaching 101.3 million steps per second on a single H100 GPU, more than three orders of magnitude faster than ARCLE at matched batch sizes, we enable the kind of systematic, multi-seed experimentation that was previously prohibitive. Our benchmarks show that while on-policy methods like PPO can uncover a learning signal, performance remains highly dependent on task structure and interface design. Together, JaxARC and its accompanying baselines provide a high-throughput foundation for evaluating future algorithms and architectures, enabling the community to move beyond isolated trials and focus on the algorithmic advances required for abstract reasoning.
