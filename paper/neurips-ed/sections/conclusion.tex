JaxARC provides an accelerator-native RL environment that makes large-scale experimentation on ARC practical. On a single H100 GPU it reaches 101.3 million steps per second, more than three orders of magnitude faster than ARCLE at matched batch sizes. We used this throughput to build RL baselines for ARC, evaluating PPO, DDQN, PQN, and REINFORCE across subsets from ARC-AGI and ConceptARC. The results show a clear advantage for PPO under this benchmark setup, large performance differences across task families, and a measurable effect of observation-interface design. Together, the environment and these baselines provide a practical starting point for RL research on ARC: future algorithms, architectures, and interface designs can be evaluated against concrete reference curves on a platform fast enough to support systematic comparison.
